How we think about risk

The intelligence comes from the relationships.

01

Evidence is not effectiveness

An artifact can prove that something exists. It does not automatically prove that a control works reliably or that the resulting business risk is acceptable.

02

Severity is not business priority

Two identical technical findings can create radically different business consequences depending on the services and dependencies behind them.

03

Context turns data into decisions

The goal is not another database of risk objects. It is a traceable explanation of what is affected, why it matters, and what should be considered first.
